Claim
-----
Holding the federation-wide record count fixed, query completion time grows with
the number of data providers.

Paper location
--------------
Section "Scalability", paragraph "Number of Providers", and Figure 4.

Procedure
---------
Splits a fixed record count across an increasing number of providers on the
Lattigo BGV backend and prints the query completion time for each federation
size. The proxy returns one aggregated result ciphertext, not one per provider.

Scale
-----
  1  1000 records over 1, 5, 10 providers               (~4 min)
  2  10000 records over 1, 10, 25 providers             (~16 min)
The paper goes to 100 providers.

Assessment
----------
Completion time should rise with the provider count while the record total holds
constant. expected/full-scale.txt lists the paper's medians up to 100 providers,
expected/scale1.txt and expected/scale2.txt the results of the two levels on our
reference machine; the slope depends on the machine's core count.
