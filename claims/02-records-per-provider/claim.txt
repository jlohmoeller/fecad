Claim
-----
Query completion time grows with the number of records per provider, and the
growth is driven by the per-patient consent layer rather than by homomorphic
evaluation: one BGV ciphertext absorbs many records, but every enrolled patient
contributes a cancellation ciphertext that the proxy must add per query.

Paper location
--------------
Section "Scalability", paragraph "Records per Provider".

Procedure
---------
Runs the reference query at one provider for several record counts on the
Lattigo BGV backend, with every patient consenting, and prints the query
completion time for each.

Scale
-----
  1  1000 and 5000 records                              (~3 min)
  2  1000, 10000 and 30000 records                      (~15 min)
The paper spans 1000 to 1000000 records; the two largest points cap the
consenting cohort at 250000 patients because one cancellation ciphertext per
patient (about 1 MiB) exceeds node-local scratch beyond that.

Assessment
----------
Completion time should rise monotonically and super-linearly in the record
count. expected/full-scale.txt lists the paper's medians, and
expected/scale1.txt and expected/scale2.txt the results of the two levels on our
reference machine.
