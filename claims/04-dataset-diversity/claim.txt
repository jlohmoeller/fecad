Claim
-----
The same protocol runs unchanged across the three cohorts. The per-cohort spread
in query completion time tracks the widest attribute domain, which sets the
degree of the Lagrange indicator polynomial.

Paper location
--------------
Section "Scalability", paragraph "Dataset Diversity", and Appendix "Dataset
Characteristics and Queries".

Procedure
---------
Runs each cohort's own reference predicate on synthetic records drawn from that
cohort's schema, and prints the query completion time.

Scale
-----
  1  1000 records per cohort                            (~2 min)
  2  10000 records per cohort                           (~12 min)

Data
----
Records are generated from the schemas in artifact/schemas/, which fix each
column's width, domain and encoding. The real cohorts (MIMIC-IV, HCUP NIS) require 
data use agreements and are not redistributable; artifact/preprocess/ holds the 
scripts that convert them, and a reviewer holding the agreements can feed the 
result in with --data-file. Supplied rows receive a consent decision drawn at
--consent-fraction, as generated rows do.

Assessment
----------
All three cohorts should complete, with times of the same order.
expected/full-scale.txt lists the paper's medians, and expected/scale1.txt and
expected/scale2.txt the results of the two levels on our reference machine.
