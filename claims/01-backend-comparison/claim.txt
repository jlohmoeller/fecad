Claim
-----
The plaintext baseline and the four FHE backends differ by orders of magnitude
in query completion time for the same consent-gated query. The ordering is
  null < Lattigo BGV < HE3DB < PatDiscover < Engorgio.

Paper location
--------------
Section "Performance", paragraph "Backend Comparison", and Figure 3.

Procedure
---------
Run the reference query at one data provider on each available backend and
prints the query completion time. Every enrolled patient consents to the query,
as in the paper.

Scale
-----
  1  1000 records, backends null/Lattigo/HE3DB          (~2 min)
  2  10000 records, all five backends                   (~5 h, Engorgio dominates)
Level 1 leaves out PatDiscover and Engorgio on purpose. Both need about half an
hour even at 1000 records, so the full ordering is only visible at level 2.
The paper reports 10000 records and the mean of 30 runs; both levels here
perform a single run.

Assessment
----------
Compare the ordering. expected/full-scale.txt lists the
paper's medians at 10000 records, expected/scale1.txt and expected/scale2.txt
list the results of the two levels on our reference machine.
Absolute values will differ from the paper, depending on the machine.
